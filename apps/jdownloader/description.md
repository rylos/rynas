JDownloader 2 downloads from file hosting sites, video sites and plain links: it collects
links from the clipboard or pasted text, splits files into parallel chunks, resumes after
an interruption, solves many captchas and unpacks archives when they are complete.

The full JDownloader desktop runs in the container and opens in the browser, behind a login
page. With a free MyJDownloader account the phone apps and browser extensions can add links
and follow the downloads, also from outside the LAN.

Finished downloads go to the chosen shared folder.
