Syncthing keeps folders in sync between computers, phones and the NAS, directly and
encrypted, without a central server. Each device chooses which folders to share with which
other devices.

Choose the shared folders Syncthing may use: each one appears inside the container as
`/shares/<share>`, and the folders you add in Syncthing live below those paths. The list can
be changed later, and folder paths inside Syncthing stay the same when a share moves to
another volume. Its own configuration, keys and database stay in the stack folder.
