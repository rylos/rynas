Frigate records IP cameras and watches their streams for people, cars and animals with local
AI object detection, so you review events instead of hours of footage. Nothing leaves your
network. It offers live view over WebRTC, a timeline, retention rules per camera and MQTT
events for Home Assistant.

Use the substream of each camera for detection and give Frigate the Intel GPU: the OpenVINO
detector on an iGPU handles several cameras on a low-power NAS.
